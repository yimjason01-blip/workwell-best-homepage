Buyer questions

FAQ should handle objections, not decorate the footer.

Is WorkWell health insurance?

No. WorkWell is a health access benefit. It can sit next to major medical, MEC, or a workforce segment that has no usable plan.

Which employees is this for?

Part-time, seasonal, hourly, opt-out, high-deductible, and cost-sensitive populations who still need fast everyday care.

How should brokers position it?

As a practical access layer for clients whose benefits strategy leaves a visible care gap. It is a new lane, not another thin discount card.

What should employers expect after launch?

Employers should expect a clear activation path, member support, and reporting around uptake, care usage, prescription savings, and service mix.
