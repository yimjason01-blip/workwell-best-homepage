Questions

The things buyers actually ask.

Is WorkWell health insurance? +

No. WorkWell is an everyday care benefit — virtual medical care, therapy, and prescription savings. It's designed to give workers practical access where a traditional plan is too expensive, too thin, or out of reach. It pairs cleanly alongside major medical where one exists.

Who is it built for? +

The workforce traditional benefits leave out: hourly, part-time, seasonal, and opt-out employees, and the small and mid-size employers who run on them. If your team turns over, works variable hours, or skips the benefits enrollment, WorkWell is built for exactly that population.

What should employers expect after launch? +

A low-burden rollout with white-glove enrollment, member support handled for you, and a usage dashboard within the first quarter. The benefit is judged on activation and retention, and those numbers are visible from day one — not buried until renewal.

How are brokers compensated? +

Through a flat fair-market-value fee under a master service agreement. WorkWell does not pay per-head or percentage-of-revenue compensation. The model is built to be clean, defensible, and compliant from the first conversation.

Who delivers the care? +

Board-certified physicians and licensed therapists, with a real member-support path behind the app. WorkWell is a care benefit delivered by clinicians, not a directory or a discount card.
